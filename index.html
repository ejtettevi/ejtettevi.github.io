About

I am a research scientist at the Council for Scientific & Industrial Research – Water Research Institute (CSIR–WRI) in Accra, where I currently serve as Acting Head of the Biomedical and Public Health Research Unit and lead the institute’s onchocerciasis and lymphatic filariasis research team — working with communities, health systems and field teams across Ghana.

My training spans three universities: a PhD in Molecular Cell Biology of Infectious Pathogens from the West African Centre for Cell Biology of Infectious Pathogens (WACCBIP), University of Ghana; an MPhil in Parasitology from KNUST; and a BSc in Molecular Biology & Biotechnology from the University of Cape Coast.

What defines my work is the bridge it builds — between the wet lab and the field, between field epidemiology and computation. Most weeks I move from bench assays to village clinics to analysis pipelines, and I believe science is only finished when it is published and reproducible in code.
